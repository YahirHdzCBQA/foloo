# Foloo Agent Instructions

Foloo follows Specification-Driven Development (SDD).

Before implementing functionality:

1. Read the business requirements.
2. Read the relevant specifications.
3. Identify affected RF, RNF, and RC requirements.
4. Do not invent business rules.
5. Document unresolved decisions instead of assuming them.

The business requirements in `docs/requirements/` are the source of truth for product scope.

Do not implement code unless the corresponding behavior has been specified.
